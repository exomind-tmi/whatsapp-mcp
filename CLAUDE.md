# whatsapp-mcp

## Commits

- Commit messages follow [Conventional Commits 1.0.0](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/en/v1.0.0/):
  `<type>(<optional scope>): <description>`, with `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `test`, `refactor`,
  `perf`, `build`, `ci` or `chore`; a breaking change adds `!` after the type or a
  `BREAKING CHANGE:` footer.
- Pull requests are squash-merged: the squash commit follows the same format and describes
  the change as a whole.
